## 6. Caveats and data quality

- AI search volume returned 0 for all eight target queries (empty result, endpoint healthy).
- The llm_mentions per-query keyword filter is fuzzy; those tallies are unreliable and excluded from conclusions.
- gpt-4o returned answers with no citable links for 6 of 8 queries; gpt-5.4 returned links for 6 of 8. Both are recorded as-is.
- AI Overview presence is volatile and personalised; this is one desktop capture at one point in time.
- savills.co.uk did not appear in any top-10 for the target queries (it reached #12 on one). knightfrank.co.uk ranks via content.knightfrank.com. interpath.com appeared once, via ChatGPT on bridging statistics.
- DataForSEO Labs domain_rank_overview is a cached database snapshot, not a live SERP read: it reports 0 position-1 keywords for us while the live SERP capture has us at #1 for the brand query. Labs figures are directional; the live SERP section is the authoritative positional record.
- No DataForSEO endpoint returned an 'unavailable' or subscription error during this capture.
