## Wokingham planning approval

Wokingham Borough Council has approved planning application 261418 for 200 residential units at Bridge Farm New Bath Road Twyford Wokingham RG10 9RU. The approved proposal is: Submission of details to comply with Condition 11 (Samples of Materials) of planning consent 212720 dated 05/07/2023 for outline application for the development of up to 200 dwellings, including 40% affordable housing and associated infrastructure, open space, biodiversity enhancements, landscaping and green infrastructure, following demolition of existing agricultural buildings. Condition 11 relates to samples and details of materials to be used in the construction of external surfaces for Phase 1.

The application was decided on 10/08/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Wokingham, Berkshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
