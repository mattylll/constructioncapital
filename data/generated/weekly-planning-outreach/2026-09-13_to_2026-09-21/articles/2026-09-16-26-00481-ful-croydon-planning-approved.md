## Croydon planning approval

London Borough of Croydon has approved planning application 26/00481/FUL for 5 residential units at Sapphire Apartments 72 Lower Addiscombe Road Croydon CR0 6AB. The approved proposal is: Erection of roof extensions and mansard third floor to existing building and outbuilding, linked by a two-storey bridge, to provide 5 additional self-contained flats; reconfiguration of Flats 2, 10 and 11; provision of communal roof terrace, play area, cycle and refuse storage; associated landscaping and external alterations (car-free).

The application was decided on 16/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Croydon, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
