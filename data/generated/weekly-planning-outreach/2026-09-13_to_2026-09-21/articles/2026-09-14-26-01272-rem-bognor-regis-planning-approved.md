## Bognor Regis planning approval

Chichester District Council has approved planning application 26/01272/REM for 19 residential units at Land North Of Laroch Post Office Lane North Mundham West Sussex. The approved proposal is: Application for approval of reserved matters appearance, landscaping, layout and scale for the construction of 19 no. dwellings and associated infrastructure with vehicular access from Post Office Lane, North Mundham, pursuant to Outline Planning Permission NM/21/02878/OUT.

The application was decided on 14/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Bognor Regis, Sussex to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
