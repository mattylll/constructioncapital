## Harrogate planning approval

North Yorkshire Council has approved planning application SELZG2025/0764/FUL for 2 residential units at Maspin Grange Hillam Common Lane Hillam West Yorkshire LS25 5HT. The approved proposal is: Conversion of existing farmhouse and associated barns to form two dwellings, including the conversion of a rear outbuilding to ancillary use, associated landscaping, creation of wildlife ponds, orchard and wildflower meadow, and installation of ecological enhancements following the demolition of agricultural buildings

The application was decided on 17/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Harrogate, North Yorkshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
