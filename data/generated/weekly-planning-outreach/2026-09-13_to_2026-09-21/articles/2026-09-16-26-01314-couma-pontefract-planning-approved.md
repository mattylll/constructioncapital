## Pontefract planning approval

Wakefield Council has approved planning application 26/01314/COUMA for 46 residential units at White Rose House West Parade Wakefield WF1 1LT. The approved proposal is: Change of use under prior notification Class MA from offices to 46 residential apartments.

The application was decided on 16/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Pontefract, West Yorkshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
