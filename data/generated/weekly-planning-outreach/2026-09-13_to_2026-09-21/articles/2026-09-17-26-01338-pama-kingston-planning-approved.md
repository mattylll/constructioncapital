## Kingston planning approval

Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames has approved planning application 26/01338/PAMA for 7 residential units at 2 Park Road Kingston Upon Thames KT2 6BN. The approved proposal is: Change of use of existing office floorspace at ground and first floors (Class E) to 7 residential units (Class C3) Prior Approval Under Schedule 2, Part 3, Class MA of the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015 (as amended)

The application was decided on 17/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Kingston, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
