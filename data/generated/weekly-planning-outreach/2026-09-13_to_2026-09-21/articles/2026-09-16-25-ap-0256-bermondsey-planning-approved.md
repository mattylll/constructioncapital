## Bermondsey planning approval

London Borough of Southwark has approved planning application 25/AP/0256 for 2 residential units at First Floor And Second Floor Flat 24 Bromar Road London Southwark SE5 8DL. The approved proposal is: Conversion of building from 2 flats to 3 flats, following construction of extensions granted under planning permission 24/AP/2954 of: Flat A - construction of a single storey side and rear extension. Flat B - construction of a first-floor roof terrace with obscure glazed balustrade, second floor rear extension and obscure glazed balustrade to existing second floor roof terrace, and construction of a rear roof dormer extension at roof level, loft conversion including the installation 2no. rooflights to the front roof slope.

The application was decided on 16/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Bermondsey, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
