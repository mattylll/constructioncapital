## Horsham planning approval

Horsham District Council has approved planning application DC/26/1106 for 1 residential unit at 171 Farhalls Crescent Horsham West Sussex RH12 4BX. The approved proposal is: Conversion of the existing detached garage into habitable living space to form a home hair salon incidental to the main dwelling, including raising the roof to increase the internal head height and replacement of the existing garage door with an entrance door.

The application was decided on 17/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Horsham, Sussex to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
