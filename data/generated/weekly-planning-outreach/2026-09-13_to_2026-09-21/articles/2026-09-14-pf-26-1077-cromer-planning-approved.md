## Cromer planning approval

North Norfolk District Council has approved planning application PF/26/1077 for 2 residential units at 19 The Driftway Sheringham Norfolk NR26 8LD. The approved proposal is: Two flat roof dormers on the west elevation within the roof of the second floor and a pitched roof dormer to the south elevation, alteration of north facing first floor bay window to a larger bay window and the creation of a new second floor balcony associated with converting the property back to a single dwelling

The application was decided on 14/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Cromer, Norfolk to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
