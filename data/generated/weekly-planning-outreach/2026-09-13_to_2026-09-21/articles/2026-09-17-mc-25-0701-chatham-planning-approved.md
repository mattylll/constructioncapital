## Chatham planning approval

Medway Council has approved planning application MC/25/0701 for 75 residential units at Land To The North Of Stoke Road And East Of Walnut Tree Grove Hoo St. Werburgh ME3 9YB. The approved proposal is: Outline application with all matters reserved for construction of up to 75 dwellings with associated works and infrastructure - demolition of existing buildings and structures

The application was decided on 17/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Chatham, Kent to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
