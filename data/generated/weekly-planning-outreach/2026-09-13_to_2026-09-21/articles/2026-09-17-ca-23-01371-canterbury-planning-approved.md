## Canterbury planning approval

Canterbury City Council has approved planning application CA/23/01371 for 9 residential units at Great Pett Farm Pett Hill Bridge Kent CT4 5AN. The approved proposal is: Erection of 9 dwellings together with access, landscape, parking and ancillary works and infrastructure following demolition of existing agricultural buildings.

The application was decided on 17/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Canterbury, Kent to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
