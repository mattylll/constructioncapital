## Filton planning approval

South Gloucestershire Council has approved planning application P25/02361/F for 80 residential units at Land At Former Lucas Works Moravian Road Kingswood South Gloucestershire BS15 8NF. The approved proposal is: Change of use of existing buildings to create a mixed use scheme of 80 dwellings and 5658 sqm of commercial space (Use Class E), with associated works.

The application was decided on 14/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Filton, Bristol to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
