## Wokingham planning approval

Wokingham Borough Council has approved planning application 253142 for 215 residential units at Parcels R7 and R13 Land at Phase 2b of the South Wokingham Strategic Development Location (SDL) Wokingham. The approved proposal is: Reserved Matters pursuant to Outline Planning Consent 191068 comprising the erection of 215 dwellings and associated infrastructure with details of appearance, landscaping, layout and scale to be considered.

The application was decided on 16/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Wokingham, Berkshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
