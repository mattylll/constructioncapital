## Tottenham planning approval

Haringey Council has approved planning application HGY/2026/1287 for 2 residential units at 5 Radley Road Tottenham London N17 6RH. The approved proposal is: Demolition of existing rear infill lean-to extension, the erection of a new single-storey rear extension and the conversion of the property into two self-contained flats (comprising one 2-bedroom flat at ground floor & one 2-bedroom flat at first floor, with associated external alterations, cycle/refuse storage and amenity space.

The application was decided on 18/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Tottenham, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
