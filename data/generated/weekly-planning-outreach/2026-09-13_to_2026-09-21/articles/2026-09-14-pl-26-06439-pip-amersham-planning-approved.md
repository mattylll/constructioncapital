## Amersham planning approval

Buckinghamshire Council has approved planning application PL/26/06439/PIP for 3 residential units at Land at 91 Mayhew Crescent and To The Rear Of 83 To 93 Mayhew Crescent High Wycombe Buckinghamshire HP13 6DF. The approved proposal is: Application for permission in principle for erection of 3 pairs of semi-detached dwellings (six in total), following the demolition of 91 Mayhew Crescent to form access, with associated remediation works.

The application was decided on 14/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Amersham, Buckinghamshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
