## Marylebone planning approval

Westminster City Council has approved planning application 25/06568/OUT for 4 residential units at 5 Lanhill Road London W9 2BP. The approved proposal is: Erection of replacement single storey rear extensions at lower ground floor level; erection of single storey rear extension at second floor level with associated terrace above at third floor level; erection of rear dormer roof extension; installation of rooflights to front roof slope; alterations to rear windows; all in association with the use of the building as 4 flats (Use Class C3).

The application was decided on 16/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Marylebone, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
