## Epsom planning approval

Epsom and Ewell Borough Council has approved planning application 25/01483/FUL for 14 residential units at Woodcote Stud Wilmerhatch Lane Epsom Surrey KT18 7UE. The approved proposal is: Demolition of all existing buildings and structures (excluding Woodcote House) and the construction of a new supported living building (Class C2), as an expansion to the retained Class C2 use at Woodcote House, and 14no residential dwellings (including 7no affordable dwellings) together with associated access, car parking and garages, landscaping and shared open space.

The application was decided on 18/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Epsom, Surrey to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
