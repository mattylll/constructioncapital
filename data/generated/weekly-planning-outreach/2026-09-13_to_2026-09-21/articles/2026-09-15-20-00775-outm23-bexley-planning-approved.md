## Bexley planning approval

London Borough of Bexley has approved planning application 20/00775/OUTM23 for 249 residential units at Erith Quarry - Parcel 3A Carlton Road Erith Kent DA8 3SR. The approved proposal is: Application for partial approval with respect to Parcel 3a of condition 27 Part A (energy strategy) pursuant to planning permission 20/00775/OUTM for the phased development of up to a total of 249 residential dwellings (use class C3) with associated works including informal and formal open space, pedestrian and cycle infrastructure, car and cycle parking (only access to be considered) and with detailed submission for parcel 3a with associated works including parking and landscaping.

The application was decided on 15/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Bexley, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
