## Harrogate planning approval

North Yorkshire Council has approved planning application HAMZB26/00525/MRC for 100 residential units at Land On The South West Side Of 11 Harkness Drive Leeming Bar North Yorkshire DL7 9BB. The approved proposal is: Application for modification of condition 2 for Substitution of revised engineering feasibility on approved application ZB24/02170/REM - Application for Approval of Reserved Matters (Appearance, Landscaping, Layout and Scale) following Outline Approval 22/01509/OUT for a 100 Per Cent Affordable Housing Scheme of 100 Dwellings and Associated Infrastructure, Access and Landscaping.

The application was decided on 15/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Harrogate, North Yorkshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
