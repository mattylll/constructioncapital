## Solihull planning approval

Solihull Metropolitan Borough Council has approved planning application PL/2026/01334/DIS for 5 residential units at Land To Rear Of Bentley Heath Cottages Tilehouse Green Lane Knowle Solihull. The approved proposal is: Discharge of condtions 3 (Materials), 9 (Trees) and 10 (Tree Barriers) of planning permission PL/2024/02781/PPFL dated 12.02.2026 for Residential development of 5 No. one bedroom alms houses, together with associated landscaping and car parking
