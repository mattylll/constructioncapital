## Bishops Stortford planning approval

East Hertfordshire District Council has approved planning application 3/26/1136/PIP for 2 residential units at Cole Green Farm West Street Hertford Hertfordshire SG13 8EZ. The approved proposal is: Conversion of barn and workshop (buildings A and D) to form two dwellings with air source heat pumps, removal of mobile home and outbuildings (buildings B, C and E), provision of access, new gardens, landscaping and associated works.

The application was decided on 16/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Bishops Stortford, Hertfordshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
