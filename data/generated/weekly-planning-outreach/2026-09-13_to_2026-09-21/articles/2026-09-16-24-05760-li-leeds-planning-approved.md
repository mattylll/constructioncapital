## Leeds planning approval

Leeds City Council has approved planning application 24/05760/LI for 6 residential units at Arncliffe 22 Shire Oak Road Headingley Leeds LS6 2DE. The approved proposal is: Listed building application for two storey extension to existing house and reconfiguration, to form an additional six flats; alterations to existing coach house forming one additional flat and restoration of exiting apartment; replace garage doors with windows; new steps and platform to side; new windows and door to rear; associated hard and soft landscaping and parking provision

The application was decided on 16/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Leeds, West Yorkshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
