## Stowmarket planning approval

Babergh & Mid Suffolk Councils has approved planning application DC/26/01548 for 9 residential units at Former Downs Garage Site Southgate Street Long Melford CO10 9HU. The approved proposal is: Full Planning Application - Erection of 9 No. Houses.

The application was decided on 14/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Stowmarket, Suffolk to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
