## Marylebone planning approval

Westminster City Council has approved planning application 26/03628/FULL for 2 residential units at 23 Meard Street London W1F 0EY. The approved proposal is: Change of use of first, second, third and mezzanine (fourth) floors from office (Class E(g)(i)) to provide two residential dwellings (one x one bedroom flat and one x studio flat) (Class C3).
