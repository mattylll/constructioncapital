## Amersham planning approval

Buckinghamshire Council has approved planning application PL/25/4022/PIP for 9 residential units at Dibden Hill Farm Dibden Hill Chalfont St Giles Buckinghamshire HP8 4RD. The approved proposal is: Permission in principle for redevelopment of buildings and hardstanding for minimum of 9 and a maximum of 9 dwellings including associated works

The application was decided on 18/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Amersham, Buckinghamshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
