## Bromsgrove planning approval

Bromsgrove & Redditch Councils has approved planning application 26/00772/LBC for 8 residential units at Church Green House 23 Church Green East Redditch Worcestershire B98 8DG. The approved proposal is: Conversion of former Nat West Bank building to 8 residential flats and two retail units
