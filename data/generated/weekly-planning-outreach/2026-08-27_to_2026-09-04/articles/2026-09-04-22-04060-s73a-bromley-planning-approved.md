## Bromley planning approval

London Borough of Bromley has approved planning application 22/04060/S73A for 2 residential units at FOXHOLLOW, MERLEWOOD DRIVE, CHISLEHURST, BR7 5LQ. The approved proposal is: Under Section 73 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990, variation of Condition 2 of planning permission reference 22/04060/FULL1 dated 19.12.2022 granted for (Demolition of ancillary residential outbuildings. Erection of two storey dwelling with associated car parking and amenity area) to allow for increased building footprint and increased external building height and internal floor heights.
