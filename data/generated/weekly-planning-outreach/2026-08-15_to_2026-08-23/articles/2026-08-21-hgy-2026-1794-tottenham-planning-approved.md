## Tottenham planning approval

Haringey Council has approved planning application HGY/2026/1794 for 3 residential units at 196 Page Green Terrace Tottenham London N15 4NP. The approved proposal is: Variation of condition 2 (approved plans) of planning permission HGY/2025/0549 for Excavation of front and rear lightwells and installation of stairs in front garden to basement level, basement extension, single storey rear extension, first and second floor rear extension, installation of waste and bike storage in front garden, reconfiguration and enlargement of existing 3 flats.
Proposed amendment: To increase depth of single storey ground floor rear extension with a conservatory.

The application was decided on 21/08/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Tottenham, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
