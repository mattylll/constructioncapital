## Basingstoke planning approval

Basingstoke & Deane Borough Council has approved planning application 26/01659/OOBC for 75 residential units at Land East Of Newbury College Monks Lane Newbury Berkshire. The approved proposal is: Reserved matters application pursuant to hybrid planning permission 22/02754/OUTMAJ for the development of 75 dwellings with associated parking, landscaping, open space (including a LEAP), infrastructure and associated works, seeking approval of layout, landscape, appearance and scale (Phase 4) Not EIA development. CASE REF: 26/01632/RESMAJ CASE OFFICER: SIAN CUTTS PLEASE NOTE THAT THE DECISION ON THIS APPLICATION WILL BE MADE BY WEST BERKS COUNCIL

The application was decided on 08/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Basingstoke, Hampshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
