## Bermondsey planning approval

London Borough of Southwark has approved planning application 26/AP/0234 for 9 residential units at 57 - 61 Union Street London Southwark SE1 1SG. The approved proposal is: Details of condition 3 (CEMP) of 24/AP/3719 Partial demolition of existing dilapidated structures within the curtilage of a listed building, installation of entrance gate between 57-59 Union Street, and associated facade repair work to the front and rear elevations of no. 59-61 Union Street. Erection of two four storey buildings, and an upwards extension to no. 57 Union Street to provide nine residential units, amenity space, refuse and bike stores, external plant, and external landscaping.

The application was decided on 09/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Bermondsey, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
