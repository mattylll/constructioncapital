## Brixton planning approval

London Borough of Lambeth has approved planning application 25/04007/FUL for 5 residential units at Rear Of 436 And 438 Streatham High Road London SW16 3PX. The approved proposal is: Demolition of the single storey retail building and erection of three storey Mews building to provide 5 self contained units (2 X 1-Bed and 3 X 2-Bed) with the provision of cycle parking, refuse/recycling storage, private amenity space and landscaping.

The application was decided on 07/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Brixton, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
