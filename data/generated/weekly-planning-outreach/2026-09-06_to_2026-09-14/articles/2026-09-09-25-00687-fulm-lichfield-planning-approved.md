## Lichfield planning approval

Lichfield District Council has approved planning application 25/00687/FULM for 3 residential units at Blue Hoarded Site Land Off Milestone Way Burntwood. The approved proposal is: Erection of 3 units comprising; Unit 3: 316 sqm(subdivided into 3 Class Ea retail units), Unit 4: 1858 sq m for retail use (Class Ea) and Unit 5: 2328sqm for retail and garden centre (Class Ea), 1 Drive Thru restaurant, including formation of new accesses off Robins Road and Morrisons service road, car parking, landscaping and associated works.

The application was decided on 09/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Lichfield, Staffordshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
