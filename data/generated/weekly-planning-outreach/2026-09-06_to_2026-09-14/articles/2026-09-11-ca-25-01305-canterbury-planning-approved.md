## Canterbury planning approval

Canterbury City Council has approved planning application CA/25/01305 for 2 residential units at 48 - 52 William Street Herne Bay Kent CT6 5PB. The approved proposal is: 2 semi-detached, two-storey dwellings, use of existing building as 2 dwellings with side dormers and rooflights, single-storey rear extension to office building, change of use of commercial unit to residential and external material alterations following demolition of the existing outbuildings.

The application was decided on 11/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Canterbury, Kent to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
