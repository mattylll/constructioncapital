## Nottingham planning approval

Nottingham City Council has approved planning application 23/02055/PFUL3 for 71 residential units at 490 Radford Road Nottingham Nottingham City NG7 7EA. The approved proposal is: Conversion and alteration of existing building (the Existing Element) and new build following demolition (the New Build Element) to provide 71 units of purpose built student accommodation (total of 136 bedrooms).

The application was decided on 10/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Nottingham, Nottinghamshire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
