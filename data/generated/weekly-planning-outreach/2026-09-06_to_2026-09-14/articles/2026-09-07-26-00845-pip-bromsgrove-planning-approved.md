## Bromsgrove planning approval

Bromsgrove & Redditch Councils has approved planning application 26/00845/PIP for 6 residential units at Land Adjacent To 22 Alcester Road Wythall Worcestershire B47 5NE. The approved proposal is: Permission in principle for erection of upto six dwellings.

The application was decided on 07/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Bromsgrove, Worcestershire to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
