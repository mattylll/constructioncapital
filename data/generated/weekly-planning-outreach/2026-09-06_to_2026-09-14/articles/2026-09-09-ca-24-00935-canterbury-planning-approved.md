## Canterbury planning approval

Canterbury City Council has approved planning application CA/24/00935 for 7 residential units at Rodneys Head North Street Herne Bay CT6 5HB. The approved proposal is: Change of use from a public house to a mixed use comprising seven residential flats and a micro pub facilitated by a two-storey rear extension, dormer windows and rooflights.

The application was decided on 09/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Canterbury, Kent to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
