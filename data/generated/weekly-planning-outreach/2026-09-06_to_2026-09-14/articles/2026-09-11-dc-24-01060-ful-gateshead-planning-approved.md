## Gateshead planning approval

Gateshead Council has approved planning application DC/24/01060/FUL for 85 residential units at St Cuthberts Clearance Area Land Between Askew Road Gateshead. The approved proposal is: The erection of up to 85 residential units (Use Class C3) with associated access, car parking, landscaping, engineering and infrastructure (Amended 13.06.2025, 15.07.2025, 07.08.2025, 02.10.2025, 17.12.2025, 22.01.2026, 07.04.2026 and 21.07.2026, additional information 24.06.2025, 25.06.2025, 26.06.2025, 29.07.2025, 07.08.2025, 17.12.2025, 22.01.2026, 07.04.2026 and 21.07.2026)

The application was decided on 11/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Gateshead, Tyne And Wear to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
