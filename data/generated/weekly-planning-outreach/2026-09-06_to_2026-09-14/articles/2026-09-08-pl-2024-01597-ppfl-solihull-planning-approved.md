## Solihull planning approval

Solihull Metropolitan Borough Council has approved planning application PL/2024/01597/PPFL for 5 residential units at Peartree Farm 2141 Warwick Road Knowle Solihull B93 0EF. The approved proposal is: Demolition of existing buildings and replacement with five dwellings, together with relocation of existing field access.

The application was decided on 08/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Solihull, West Midlands to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
