## Croydon planning approval

London Borough of Croydon has approved planning application 25/02461/FUL for 2 residential units at The Drum And Monkey 7 Gloucester Road Croydon CR0 2DH. The approved proposal is: Erection of 2 storeys above the existing pub, including 2 storey rear extension and expansion of basement, to accommodate 2 flats, along with the retention of the ground floor pub. Demolition of outbuilding at the rear and erection of a 2 storey dwelling. (3 units total). Associated external cycle and refuse storage.

The application was decided on 07/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Croydon, Greater London to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
