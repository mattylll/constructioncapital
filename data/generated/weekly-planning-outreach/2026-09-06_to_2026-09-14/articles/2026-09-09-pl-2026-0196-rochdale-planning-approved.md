## Rochdale planning approval

Rochdale Borough Council has approved planning application PL/2026/0196 for 7 residential units at Land at Egerton Street (Former New Jerusalem Church), Middleton, M24 4QD. The approved proposal is: Erection of seven residential dwellings (Use Class C3) with associated access works including formation of a new dropped kerb, parking, bin storage, landscaping and private amenity space.

The application was decided on 09/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Rochdale, Greater Manchester to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
