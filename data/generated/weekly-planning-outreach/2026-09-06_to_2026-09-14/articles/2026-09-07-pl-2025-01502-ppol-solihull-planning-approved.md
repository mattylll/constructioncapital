## Solihull planning approval

Solihull Metropolitan Borough Council has approved planning application PL/2025/01502/PPOL for 110 residential units at Land West Of Rumbush Lane Solihull. The approved proposal is: Outline planning permission is sought for the erection of up to 110 dwellings, provision of a sports pitch, the creation of associated vehicular and pedestrian accesses onto Rumbush Lane, pedestrian footway to Dewberry Road, highway improvements, parking, landscaping, drainage features, open space, and associated infrastructure (All matters reserved except for access into the site from Rumbush Lane (southern access only) and Dewberry Road).

The application was decided on 07/09/2026. Construction Capital has reviewed the local market data for Solihull, West Midlands to provide a high-level funding view for the site.
